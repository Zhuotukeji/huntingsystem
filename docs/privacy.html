觅才 BOSS 寻访助手隐私政策

本政策说明 Chrome 扩展在招聘人员主动操作时如何处理账号配置、招聘任务、候选人页面和简历信息。

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 21 日最近更新：2026 年 8 月 21 日发布者：Zhuotukeji
重要说明 本扩展不会自动浏览 BOSS、读取 Cookie 或密码、绕过验证码，也不会自动发送消息或作出录用、淘汰决定。候选人页面分析和简历扫描仅在招聘人员明确点击并确认处理依据后进行。

1. 适用范围与产品用途

本政策适用于“觅才 BOSS 寻访助手”Chrome 扩展。扩展的单一用途是辅助企业招聘人员手动执行人才搜索任务、判断当前可见候选人页面、扫描长简历的关键帧、生成可人工审核的沟通草稿并回填招聘结果。

正式模式连接招聘企业自行运行的本地后台。商店审核模式仅使用扩展内置的合成数据，不需要 BOSS 账号、本地后台或 AI 服务，不处理真实候选人信息。

2. 我们处理的信息

信息类别具体内容用途
账号与连接配置工作邮箱、本地后台地址、临时会话令牌、审核模式偏好连接企业后台、保持登录和显示正确工作区
招聘任务数据人才画像、搜索词、任务状态、结果人数、符合画像人数、有效沟通人数和人工备注执行招聘工作流并改进后续搜索建议
当前页面内容招聘人员主动分析时当前可见的 BOSS 候选人结果页截图基于可见证据提供候选人匹配辅助判断
简历关键帧招聘人员开始扫描后，每次主动点击“截取当前帧”产生并最终选取的最多 10 张关键帧提取姓名、任职经历、教育、技能、项目和公司事实，合并为结构化简历
技术状态扩展版本、后台健康状态、AI 配置状态和操作错误信息连接检测、兼容性校验和错误提示

原始页面可能短暂包含头像、电话、邮箱、微信、证件号等信息。系统要求 AI 不返回非必要身份字段，并在结构化简历合并前移除电话、邮箱、微信、证件号、年龄、性别、婚育和照片描述等非必要信息。

3. 信息如何被触发和使用

  • 扩展不会在后台持续监控页面。截图分析和简历帧采集都必须由招聘人员点击侧边栏中的明确按钮触发。
  • 开始真实简历扫描前，招聘人员必须填写来源及处理依据并确认当前招聘用途。
  • 候选人判断仅提供证据、评分和未知项，最终决定由招聘人员作出。
  • 沟通内容只生成可复制草稿，扩展不会自动发送。
  • 我们不会将信息用于广告、跨站跟踪、出售数据、信用评估或与招聘无关的用途。

4. 存储、传输和保留

Chrome 本地存储

扩展在 Chrome 本地存储中保存后台地址、工作邮箱、审核模式偏好和有效期最长 7 天的会话令牌。插件访问码只用于向本地后台换取会话，不由扩展持久化。

截图与关键帧

截图和关键帧只在当前侧边栏内存及请求处理期间短暂存在，不写入 Chrome 存储、后台数据库、日志或视频文件。成功、取消、关闭侧边栏或发生错误后，扩展会释放这些画面数据。

结构化招聘数据

识别后的结构化简历、任职关系、公司事实、技能和学习结果保存在招聘企业自行控制的后台数据库中，按照该企业的招聘数据制度保留，直至企业管理员根据适用制度和请求进行删除或更正。

5. 信息接收方

  • 招聘企业自行运行的本地后台：用于身份验证、任务同步、结构化简历保存和招聘学习。
  • 招聘企业配置的 Sub2API/AI 服务：仅在启用相关功能且招聘人员主动操作时，用于当前页面判断和简历关键帧结构化。该服务的保留和安全条款由招聘企业与其所选服务提供方的协议及配置决定。
  • 法律要求：仅在适用法律、监管要求或有效法律程序要求时披露。

开发者不会将候选人或招聘数据出售给第三方，也不会提供给广告网络或数据经纪商。

6. Chrome 权限说明

  • activeTab：在用户主动操作且 Chrome 已授予当前页临时权限时，确认允许捕获当前可见标签页。
  • tabCapture：按已经校验的标签页 ID 定向取得当前 BOSS 页面或内置演示页的媒体流，不使用屏幕、窗口或插件侧边栏作为扫描来源。标签页流仅在本次扫描期间保存在内存中。
  • tabs：确认当前页面属于 BOSS 或内置演示页，并在用户点击后打开相应页面。
  • sidePanel：显示扩展的主要任务、扫描和设置界面。
  • storage：保存连接配置、会话和审核模式偏好。
  • localhost/127.0.0.1 主机权限：只用于连接招聘企业自行运行的本地后台。

扩展不包含远程执行代码。所有可执行 JavaScript 均随扩展包发布，网络响应只作为数据处理，不会被当作代码执行。

7. 安全措施

扩展采用最小权限、明确用户触发、会话令牌哈希校验、服务端密钥加密、来源限制、候选人一致性校验和非必要敏感字段清理等措施。没有任何系统可以保证绝对安全，招聘企业仍应限制后台访问、配置可信 AI 服务并执行内部招聘数据制度。

8. 用户选择与数据权利

招聘人员可以不启动截图或扫描、随时停止共享、退出插件、清除扩展本地数据或卸载扩展。候选人或招聘人员如需访问、更正或删除已保存的结构化信息，应联系负责招聘的企业或通过 Chrome Web Store 商品详情页中已验证的发布者联系邮箱提出请求。

9. 儿童隐私

本扩展面向企业招聘人员，不面向儿童，也不用于主动收集儿童个人信息。

10. 政策更新与联系我们

如本扩展的数据处理方式发生实质变化，我们会更新本页面的“最近更新”日期，并在适用情况下提供额外通知。

Privacy Policy for Hunting BOSS Sourcing Assistant

Effective date: August 21, 2026   Last updated: August 21, 2026   Publisher: Zhuotukeji

This Chrome extension has one purpose: to assist employer-authorized recruiters with manually executing sourcing tasks, assessing visible candidate pages, scanning long resumes into temporary keyframes, producing reviewable outreach drafts, and submitting recruiting feedback.

The extension does not automatically browse BOSS, read cookies or passwords, bypass verification, send messages, or make hiring or rejection decisions. Candidate analysis and resume scanning occur only after an explicit user action and confirmation of the recruiting purpose.

Information processed

  • Account and connection data: work email, local backend address, review-mode preference, and an expiring session token.
  • Recruiting workflow data: job profiles, search terms, task status, result counts, qualified counts, effective conversation counts, and recruiter notes.
  • Visible page content: a screenshot of the currently visible BOSS candidate results page when the recruiter explicitly requests analysis.
  • Resume keyframes: up to ten frames selected from screenshots explicitly triggered by the recruiter clicking “截取当前帧” in the side panel.
  • Technical status: extension version, local backend health, configured AI status, and operation errors.

Raw pages may briefly contain photos or contact and identity details. The system instructs the AI not to return unnecessary identity data and removes phone numbers, email addresses, messaging IDs, government identifiers, age, sex, marital or parental status, and photo descriptions before merging structured resume data.

Collection, use, and user control

  • The extension does not continuously monitor pages in the background. Screenshot analysis and resume-frame capture each require an explicit side-panel button click by the recruiter.
  • Real resume scans require the recruiter to record and confirm a lawful recruiting basis.
  • Candidate assessments provide evidence, scores, and unknowns only; a human recruiter makes the final decision.
  • Outreach is generated only as a copyable draft and is never sent automatically.
  • Data is not used for advertising, cross-site tracking, sale, credit decisions, or purposes unrelated to recruiting.

Storage and retention

Chrome local storage contains the backend address, work email, review-mode preference, and a session token valid for no more than seven days. The access code is exchanged for a session and is not persisted by the extension.

Screenshots and keyframes exist only temporarily in side-panel memory and during request processing. They are not written to Chrome storage, backend files, the backend database, logs, or video files. They are released after success, cancellation, side-panel closure, or an error.

Extracted resume records, employment relationships, organization facts, skills, and learning results are retained in the employer-controlled backend under the employer's recruiting data policy until an administrator removes or corrects them under applicable policy and requests.

Recipients and service providers

  • Employer-operated local backend: authentication, task synchronization, structured resume storage, and recruiting learning.
  • Employer-configured Sub2API/AI service: visible-page assessment and resume keyframe extraction only when enabled and explicitly initiated. Retention and security are governed by the employer's agreement and configuration with its selected provider.
  • Legal requirements: disclosure only when required by applicable law, regulation, or valid legal process.

The publisher does not sell candidate or recruiting data and does not provide it to advertising networks or data brokers.

Chrome permissions

  • activeTab: preferentially capture the current visible tab after a user action when Chrome has granted temporary access to that tab.
  • Tab-sharing fallback: if temporary tab access is unavailable, Chrome asks the user to choose a source. The extension accepts only a Chrome tab, rejects windows and entire screens, and requires first-frame confirmation. The stream remains in memory only for the current scan.
  • tabs: verify that the current page is a BOSS page or the bundled demo and open a page after a user action.
  • sidePanel: provide the primary tasks, scanning, and settings workspace.
  • storage: retain connection, session, and review-mode settings.
  • localhost/127.0.0.1 host access: connect only to the employer-operated local backend.

The extension does not use remote executable code. All executable JavaScript is packaged with the extension, and network responses are handled only as data.

Security, rights, and contact

Controls include least-privilege permissions, explicit user initiation, hashed session-token validation, encrypted server-side secrets, source restrictions, candidate-consistency checks, and removal of unnecessary sensitive fields. No system can guarantee absolute security; employers must also restrict backend access, configure a trusted AI provider, and apply their internal recruiting data policies.

Recruiters may decline to start a capture, stop sharing at any time, sign out, clear extension data, or uninstall the extension. Candidates and recruiters seeking access, correction, or deletion of structured information should contact the recruiting employer or use the publisher's verified contact email on the Chrome Web Store listing.

This extension is intended for enterprise recruiters, not children, and is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information.
